# Network Topology Notes

## Principles

- Ethernet wherever practical.
- Avoid Wi-Fi for fixed infrastructure.
- Avoid battery sensors where wired preparation is possible.
- Prefer local APIs and open protocols.

## Prepare Ethernet For

- technical room rack
- Home Assistant host
- PV inverter
- heat recovery ventilation
- TV/media area
- office desk
- ceiling Wi-Fi access points
- future garage
- future wallbox
- cameras

## Cameras

Prefer PoE cameras with local RTSP/ONVIF support. Avoid cloud-only cameras for security-critical use.
