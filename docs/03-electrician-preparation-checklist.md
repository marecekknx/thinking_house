# Electrician Preparation Checklist

This checklist is intentionally focused on preparation. Exact protection devices, breaker sizing, RCD/RCBO strategy and final compliance remain the electrician's and electrical designer's responsibility.

## Technical Room

- Main distribution board location confirmed.
- Space reserved for KNX and automation modules.
- Space reserved for future FVE / battery integration.
- Space reserved for network rack or cabinet.
- Ethernet available near KNX IP router.
- Backup circuit distribution concept prepared.
- Cable trays or ducts planned with reserve.
- All cables labeled on both ends.

## KNX Bus

- KNX TP cable to every planned button position.
- KNX TP cable to presence detector positions.
- KNX TP cable to future CO2 sensor positions.
- KNX TP cable to bathroom humidity sensor positions.
- KNX TP cable to weather station position.
- KNX TP cable reserved for future garage.
- KNX TP cable reserved for future covered terrace.

## DALI Lighting

- DALI wiring prepared before ceilings are closed.
- 230 V + DALI available at DALI fixture/driver positions.
- LED strip drivers placed in service-accessible locations.
- No LED driver permanently hidden without access.
- DALI branches documented.
- Non-DALI circuits separated and documented.

## Blinds

- Motor cable from each external blind to distribution board.
- KNX/weather station cable route prepared.
- Wind protection concept confirmed.
- Terrace door contact prepared for blind blocking.

## Electric Floor Heating

- Separate heating zones defined.
- Heating mats/cables not switched directly by KNX actuator.
- KNX actuator outputs control power-contactor coils.
- Floor probes installed in replaceable conduits.
- Independent floor temperature limits planned.

## Doors And Windows

- Magnetic contacts prepared for all exterior doors.
- Window contacts prepared according to final window schedule.
- Tilt/open detection to be decided per window.
- Cables routed either centrally to binary inputs or locally to interface modules.

## Ventilation

- Ethernet or Modbus cable route prepared to heat recovery ventilation.
- KNX or auxiliary control path reserved for Boost/Comfort/Away if needed.
- Filter/alarm signal integration considered.

## Air Conditioning

- Cable routes prepared for max. two indoor units.
- Data/communication path prepared according to final AC manufacturer.
- Space reserved for KNX/Modbus/IP gateway if required.

## PV, Battery And Wallbox

- Ethernet to PV inverter area.
- Data route prepared for battery/inverter ecosystem.
- Backup circuit separation planned.
- Future wallbox cable route to garage prepared.
- Ethernet to future wallbox prepared.
- KNX to future garage prepared.

## Future Outdoor Systems

- Conduit to future garage.
- Conduit to covered terrace.
- Conduit to garden/borehole area.
- Conduit to future pool technology area.
- Spare conduits installed where trenching will be hard later.
